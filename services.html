Food & Nutrition Support Our organization distributes monthly food parcels
to over 150 families and provides school lunch programs to ensure children have access to nutritious meals.




Donation & Fundraising Events

We organize quarterly donation drives, charity walks, and fundraising galas to mobilize community support. These events not only raise essential funds for our programs but also create opportunities for individuals and businesses to contribute resources, time, and expertise toward our mission.
